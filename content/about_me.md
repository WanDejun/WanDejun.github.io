# About Me
---

I'm a direct-track Ph.D. student (Class of 2023) at the College of Computer Science and Technology, Harbin Engineering University.

My current work focuses on RISC-V architecture emulator, and AI compiler optimization. In addition, I have a strong interest in the Linux kernel, embedded systems, and Loongson (LoongArch). I am also actively advocating for and promoting the RISC-V architecture in research and educational settings.

During my undergraduate studies, I engaged in competitive programming and won a Silver Medal in the ACM-ICPC Regional Contest. I have also frequently contributed to problem setting and examination administration for provincial and national competitions, served as a coach for algorithm contests, and promoted computer programming and algorithms among primary and secondary school students.
